# A platform where people signal what they need — and get insight into how others see it.

Servimos is a community-driven platform for sharing needs related to products, services, and changes people want to see. Posts gather votes and structured feedback from real people. The platform processes that feedback to help reveal what people need, what they currently do about it, and what a better solution could look like.

---

## Who it's for

**Community Members** — Anyone with an opinion. Join communities you care about, vote on ideas, and share feedback about what you currently use and the problems you face.

**Entrepreneurs** — Founders and creators looking to understand demand. Post a need, collect votes, and use the platform's analysis to turn scattered feedback into structured market insights.

---

## How it works

### 1. Join a community

Browse communities by topic and search by name or interest.

### 2. Post about a need

Describe a gap: a product that doesn't exist, a service that falls short, or a change you want to see. What matters is the need itself.

### 3. Collect votes and feedback

People can answer the question **"How much would you pay for this?"** and provide optional feedback about the solutions they currently use and the problems they face.

### 4. Get insights

The platform processes the collected feedback to help identify:

* **What people need** — the main pain points emerging from the feedback
* **What they use today** — existing solutions and workarounds
* **What could be different** — opportunities for a better solution
* **Willingness to pay** — how responses differ across groups of people

Entrepreneurs can request deeper analysis where available.

### 5. Connect with other entrepreneurs

Discover other entrepreneurs interested in the same needs and connect with them to discuss potential opportunities.

---

## The Y-Z-U Framework

Servimos can use the **Y-Z-U** framework to help entrepreneurs structure their understanding of a potential market:

> **You need to make an X that would solve Y better than Z, with the help of U.**

* **Y** — The pain point: what people actually struggle with
* **Z** — The current alternative: what they're already doing
* **U** — The unique feature: what a new solution could offer that existing alternatives don't

The framework uses collected feedback to help formulate a potential solution. Its output should be treated as an analytical aid, not a guarantee that a business idea will succeed.

Servimos uses a moderation system to review content. Community moderation arrangements may vary between communities.
