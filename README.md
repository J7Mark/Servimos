# Servimos

**A platform where people signal what they need — and get insight into how others see it.**

Servimos is a community-driven platform for sharing needs in products, services, or changes. Each post gathers votes and structured feedback from real people. The platform processes that feedback and delivers concentrated insight: what people actually need, what they currently do about it, and what a better solution could look like.

---

## Who it's for

**Community Members** — Anyone with an opinion. Join communities you care about, vote on ideas, and share feedback on what you currently use and what problems you face.

**Entrepreneurs** — Founders and creators looking to understand real demand. Post a need, collect votes, and let the platform turn scattered feedback into structured market intelligence. Connect with other entrepreneurs interested in the same post.

---

## How it works

### 1. Join a community
Browse communities by topic. Search by name or interest.

### 2. Post about a need
Describe a gap — a product that doesn't exist, a service that falls short, a change you want to see. It doesn't matter who you are or what your angle is. What matters is the need.

### 3. Collect votes
People answer one question: **"How much would you pay for this?"** — plus optional feedback on what they currently use and what problems they face with existing solutions.

### 4. Get insight
The platform processes feedback into concentrated analysis:
- **What people need** — the core pain points, distilled from all feedback
- **What they use today** — the current solutions people rely on
- **What could be different** — the unique angle a better solution might offer
- Willingness-to-pay breakdown across feedback groups

Deeper analysis is available as an option for entrepreneurs.

### 5. Connect with relevant businesses
You can connect to other entrepreneurs who might be interested in the same post as you. Pay a small fee to chat with them.

---

## The Y-Z-U Framework

The platform optionally applies the **Y-Z-U** framework to help entrepreneurs understand their market:

> **You need to make an X that would solve Y better than Z, with the help of: U**

- **Y** — The pain point: what people actually struggle with
- **Z** — The current alternative: what they're already doing
- **U** — The unique feature: what your solution would do that nothing else does

Each answer is carefully composed from real feedback and reviewed for accuracy before being shown.
